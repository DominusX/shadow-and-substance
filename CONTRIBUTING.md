# 🗺️ Contributing to shadow-and-substance

Thank you for seeking to calibrate this archive. This repository serves as an objective ledger documenting instances of selective outrage, ideological overreach, and technical misdirection against independent creators. 

To maintain the highest level of academic rigor and ensure we do not descend into standard internet gossip, emotional venting, or tribal culture wars, all contributions must strictly adhere to the structural protocols outlined below.

---

## 🛑 The Core Directives

1. **Facts Over Feelings:** Submissions must be entirely grounded in verifiable development realities, storefront policies, or public developer disclosures. Do not speculate on a creator's unstated motivations or an audience's personal emotions.
2. **Structural Alignment:** Every new case study must seamlessly map to the exact structural metrics established in the primary README: *The Substance*, *The Shadow*, *The Concession/Vindication*, and *The Structural Fallout*.
3. **No Bad-Faith Partisanship:** This archive is built to defend creative sovereignty and technical literacy. We do not host submissions that seek to attack developers for legitimate consumer criticism regarding functionality, performance optimization, or broken game loops. 

---

## 📋 Case Study Markdown Template

When opening a Pull Request to propose an entry under Section 2 (`Case Studies`), you must copy, paste, and fully populate the following raw markdown block. Replace all bracketed text (`[like this]`) with your factual research data.

```markdown
### Case Model [XX]: *[Game Title]* ([Developer/Studio Name])

#### I. The Substance (The Structural Choice)
[Provide a clear, technical description of what the developer actually did. Detail the exact nature of the assets, engine workflows, or tools utilized. Explicitly state the human craftsmanship involved in the creative execution.]

#### II. The Shadow (The Selective Outrage)
[Document the specific nature of the audience backlash. Detail the false arguments, low-effort buzzwords, or ideological double standards weaponized by the community during the harassment or review-bombing campaign.]

#### III. The Deflection / Vindication / Error
[Explain the turning point. Did the developer make a fatal error and cave to the pressure (Concession)? Or was the claim definitively dismantled by technical, experienced observation (Vindication)? State exactly what occurred.]

#### IV. The Structural Fallout
[Analyze the real-world, long-term impact on the game's ecosystem, community health, storefront rating, or developer retention. Detail how the target volatility or the Backrooms Effect manifested within the project's lifecycle.]
```

---

## 🪵 Submission Review Workflow

1. **File an Issue First:** Before drafting a massive markdown expansion, open an issue labeled `case-proposal` summarizing the studio, the game, and the core structural contradiction. 
2. **Verification Verification:** Provide direct links to verifiable sources (e.g., Steam store disclosures, developer patch notes, archived community forum pins, or direct commentary from experienced tech analysts).
3. **The Filtration Gate:** Submissions that lean on emotional internet slang, contain sweeping generalizations about entire geographical regions, or fail to follow the mandatory structural layout will be closed without adjustment.
